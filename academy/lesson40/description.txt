Lesson 40 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, the fourth lesson of the Bash scripting masterclass. Servers produce text: logs, lists and configuration files. You learn six tools for searching and reshaping it, each in a real demo on Ubuntu: grep (with the count, invert, line number and extended pattern options), cut and sort (columns, and sorting text or numbers, with a count of repeats), sed (search and replace, and when the dash i option changes the file), awk (fields, conditions and an average) and find (by name and age, with the exec option). A small log report then joins the tools with pipes. The lesson ends with common myths, a six-point pass or fail rubric and six tasks to do in your own virtual machine.
