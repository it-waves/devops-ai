Lesson 150 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, the sixth lesson of the DevOps Fundamentals domain. Without numbers, improving how a team delivers software is guesswork. In this lesson you learn the four key DevOps metrics from the DORA research: deployment frequency and lead time for changes (speed), and change failure rate and time to restore (stability). You also learn why to read them together, why they describe a team and never a person, and why a number that becomes a target gets gamed. In the live demo, a real terminal calculates all four metrics from a deploy log for two different teams, so you can see a healthy team and a struggling one side by side.

Demo files: github.com/it-waves/devops-ai, in academy/lesson150-demo (deploys.csv, deploys_slow.csv, metrics.sh and a README with the answers). You need a shell and awk. This domain has no lab on purpose: it is about ideas and decisions, so the practice is a plain terminal exercise with a self-check.

Honest notes: the terminal demo is a real recording of a real shell running a small script written for this lesson. The deploy logs are example data, and metrics.sh is a teaching stand-in: real teams read these numbers from their pipeline and incident tools. The narration is synthetic speech. The hook, plan, why, speed, stability, together, task, files, self-check and recap screens are labelled illustrations, not captures.
