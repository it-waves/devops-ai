Lesson 171 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, SonarQube domain. A pipeline goes red at two in the morning and the log has two thousand lines. Where do you look? Not at the last line. You learn one habit, read the FIRST error, and a five-question method: which stage failed, what is the first error, which hop in the chain is it, test that hop alone, change one thing. Then you watch four real failures happen and get fixed live: a wrong port in the Jenkins SonarQube server entry, a SonarQube server that is stopped (same message, different cause), a missing web hook that makes the gate stage time out with no error, and a quality gate that says no because coverage is 59.1% against a 62% rule.
