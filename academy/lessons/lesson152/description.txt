Lesson 152 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, the eighth lesson of the DevOps Fundamentals domain. A list of fifty improvement ideas rarely improves anything, but a small plan with a number at the start, one change, and a number at the end does. In this lesson you learn why improvement plans fail (vague, too much at once, no numbers, no people), the six parts of a practical plan (baseline, bottleneck, one change, target, measure, risk), and how to turn the metrics from lesson 150 and the bottleneck from lesson 151 into a worked plan for CloudCart. In the live demo, a real terminal checks a weak plan (six fixes needed), then a good plan (ready), and then you write and check your own.

Objectives covered: MC16-O08, a practical DevOps improvement plan for CloudCart.

Demo files: github.com/it-waves/devops-ai, in academy/lesson152-demo (baseline.txt, plan_weak.txt, plan_good.txt, plan.sh and a README with the answers). You need a shell and awk. This domain has no lab on purpose: it is about ideas and decisions, so the practice is a plain terminal exercise with a self-check.

Honest notes: the terminal demo is a real recording of a real shell running a small script written for this lesson. The CloudCart numbers are example data, and plan.sh is a teaching stand-in: it checks that a plan has the right parts, not that the plan is wise. The narration is synthetic speech. The hook, plan, why-plans-fail, six-parts, CloudCart, what-comes-next, task, files, self-check and recap screens are labelled illustrations, not captures.
