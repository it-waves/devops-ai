Lesson 176 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, the mastery lesson of the Nexus Repository domain. Four builds are red, with four different numbers, and the habit that fixes them all is reading the first error, not the last line. In this lesson we break our own Nexus Repository four ways, on a real server with a real browser and a real terminal, and fix each one: a wrong password (401), a role that is too small (403), a version that was never published (404, plus stale maven-metadata.xml and the task that rebuilds it), and a repository someone left offline (503). Then a script checks seven facts about your artifact pipeline, you fall into the trap of a password left in the code, remove it, reach seven of seven, keep the results as evidence, and clean up.

Objectives covered: MC19-O08 (troubleshooting Nexus by reading the first error) and the mastery evidence for the whole Nexus Repository domain (independently verifiable scenario, results, troubleshooting and cleanup).

Lab files: github.com/it-waves/devops-ai in the folder academy/lab: lab176.sh and shortcuts176.sh. Load the short names with source shortcuts176.sh, then run lab up, lab check and lab down. Set NEXUS_ADMIN (admin:password), NEXUS_USER and NEXUS_PASSWORD (your deployer) in your own shell, never in a file. Run lab check inside your project folder.

Honest notes: the browser and terminal are a real continuous recording of Sonatype Nexus Repository Community Edition 3.98.1 on a throwaway lab server, continuing from Lessons 173 to 175. Passwords shown belong to that throwaway server. In the terminal, why prints only the lines that matter from the Maven output, and nx is a short alias for curl with the deployer login. The coloured boxes and labels on top are overlays added for teaching. The narration is synthetic speech. The plan, domain, scenario, mistakes, lab-info, your-turn and recap screens are labelled illustrations, not captures. Use your own machine only.
