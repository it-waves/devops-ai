Lesson 179 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, the CI/CD Engineering mastery lab. Reliability and troubleshooting, then proof. You measure the Cloud Cart pipeline (success rate and average duration), break it three ways on purpose, fix each failure from its first error, and then a script proves the whole domain with seven independent checks, saves the evidence, and cleans up.

The three failures, all in real Jenkins: a stale deployer password gives a 401 at Publish to Nexus and is fixed in Jenkins Credentials; a mistyped version gives a 404 at Release and is fixed by asking Nexus which versions exist; a stopped dev Tomcat gives "no answer on port" at Deploy to dev and is fixed by starting the server. Then lab check finds a forgotten password in an old script (6 of 7), you remove it and treat the password as leaked, and lab check passes 7 of 7.
