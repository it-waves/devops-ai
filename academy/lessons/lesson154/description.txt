Lesson 154 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, the first lesson of the Jenkins domain. Jenkins is the server that watches your code, builds it, tests it and reports the result. Before you install anything, you learn how it is put together (controller, agents, executors, the build queue and the Jenkins home folder), how a build lives from trigger to result, and why plugins matter. Then a continuous live recording of a real Jenkins 2.580 LTS walks the whole screen for beginners: header, search, Manage Jenkins, plugins, nodes, a first freestyle job, the build queue, a pipeline with stages, and a failing build you learn to read.

Objectives covered: MC17-O01 Jenkins architecture (controller, agents, executors) and a tour of the web UI.

Lab files: github.com/it-waves/devops-ai, in academy/lab (lab154.sh and shortcuts154.sh) and academy/lab154-jobs (three example jobs). Needs Java 21 and internet. Run "lab up", sign in at localhost:8080 as admin with the lab password shown in the script, then "lab down" removes everything. Use your own machine only.

Honest notes: the Jenkins screens are a real continuous recording of Jenkins 2.580 LTS running in a lab. The coloured boxes and labels on top of the recording are overlays added for teaching. In the recording, plugins were installed from the Jenkins Maven repository through a local update centre because of sandbox network limits; the screens you see are the same. Jenkins ran as a normal non-root user. The example jobs are teaching stand-ins. The narration is synthetic speech. The hook, plan, architecture, life-of-a-build, files, your-turn and recap screens are labelled illustrations, not captures.
