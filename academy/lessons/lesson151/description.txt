Lesson 151 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, the seventh lesson of the DevOps Fundamentals domain. A team can buy faster servers and hire more developers, and releases still take days, because one slow step sets the pace for the whole process. In this lesson you learn what a bottleneck is, the difference between work time and waiting time, flow efficiency and the value stream map, how to find the bottleneck (list the steps, measure work and wait, compare) and the usual suspects (slow reviews, manual approvals, flaky tests, waiting for environments, big batches), and the three rules for fixing it: one at a time, measure again, and expect the bottleneck to move. In the live demo, a real terminal measures a six-stage pipeline, finds the slow step, applies the fix, and shows the bottleneck moving to a new stage.
