Lesson 177 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp. A pipeline is the road from a commit to production, and the most important rule on that road is simple: build once, promote many. In this lesson you watch a real Jenkins pipeline build CloudCart a single time, publish the war file to Nexus as an immutable release, and deploy it to dev. A second job then promotes the very same file to stage and production, with no rebuild, and you prove it by comparing the SHA-1 checksum in each environment. Then you ship a change, a new banner, and see version 1.0.2 flow through while production stays on 1.0.1, on purpose.
