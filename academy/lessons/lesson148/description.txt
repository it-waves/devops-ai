Lesson 148 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, the fourth lesson of the DevOps Fundamentals domain. A version passes every test, then breaks in production, because it was rebuilt on the way. In this lesson you learn the build artifact (one package with a name, a version and a fingerprint), the golden rule "build once", the artifact repository and why versions are immutable, environments (dev, test, staging, production) and why settings live outside the artifact, release promotion through gates, and rollback, the safety net that makes fast releases safe. In the live demo, a real terminal rebuilds by mistake (three different fingerprints), then builds once and promotes the same artifact to dev, test and prod with each environment's own settings, then a tampered artifact is refused, and a clean rebuild is verified and promoted.
