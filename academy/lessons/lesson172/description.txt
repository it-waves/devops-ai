Lesson 172 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, SonarQube mastery check. Prove you can run the whole quality pipeline yourself. You build a Cloud Cart gate with a coverage rule of 62 percent, connect SonarQube to Jenkins with a web hook, and watch the pipeline FAIL because coverage is 59.1%. Then you fix it by adding a test, watch it pass, find a leaked token in the code, remove it and revoke it. The lab has seven independent checks. Seven of seven is mastery.
