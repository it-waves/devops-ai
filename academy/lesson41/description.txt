Lesson 41 of the DevOps Engineering Bootcamp, the fifth lesson of the Bash scripting masterclass. Every command has three channels: standard in, standard out and standard error. Once you control them you can save output, hide noise and join commands. You learn them in real demos on Ubuntu: the three streams and their numbers, redirection with greater than, double greater than, less than and two greater than, the exit status, pipes and tee, and safe temporary files with mktemp. A small job then saves a report to one file and keeps the errors in another. The lesson ends with common myths, a six-point pass or fail rubric and six tasks to do in your own virtual machine.
